## Metadata and limits

Book metadata uses Open Library Search/Works/Covers and Google Books as fallback, separately from MusicBrainz/LRCLIB song lookup. Matching checks normalized title tokens and a known author. Embedded artwork takes priority; downloaded covers are bounded and checked as images before caching. Complete metadata is cached for 30 days; partial results and confirmed misses are retried after one day. The cache version forces a lookup after updating the previous metadata algorithm. Google Books is also tried when Open Library finds a book without its author or cover. Network/provider failures remain eligible for retry at the next scan/start; manual refresh bypasses the age check.

Illustrated fallback covers are rendered once at 420 × 588 pixels with Android Canvas on the IO dispatcher and cached as PNGs. Their signatures include the book identity, title, and author, so an enriched name gets a refreshed cover. All player surfaces use the same cover resolver. Entrance, press, chapter-mark, artwork, and palette transitions are finite. During playback the music record rotates, the active book cover sways, and an open-book icon turns pages. The phase clock updates at most 30 times per second and suspends on pause, background lifecycle, or disposal; the hidden home screen is explicitly inactive beneath the full player. Rotation and translation are applied in graphics layers rather than relaying out the library. Android's disabled-animation setting is respected. The book player remaining-time calculation uses minute precision and remembers the total duration of future chapters.

The automated playback fixtures deliberately use cached misses to keep testing offline. A live Open Library request failed TLS negotiation from this environment; Google Books returned HTTP 429. Therefore a successful live metadata/cover lookup was not demonstrated in this session. Public API documentation was checked; no API credentials are embedded. The local bookshelf remains usable when providers fail or do not contain a matching edition.

Physical Android 14 checks cover the reported library and artwork motion; no CPU, battery, frame-time benchmark, or comprehensive Android 10 testing is claimed. Single-file M4B audiobooks appear as one playable file; embedded M4B chapter-table extraction is not implemented. Folder grouping does not move or rename audio files on disk.
